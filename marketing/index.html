Questions

Before you call.

Do employees need to install anything?

No. It's a phone call to a regular number. No app, no login, no calendar invite.

What if they don't finish in one call?

Most calls run 15–30 minutes. If something's cut short, you can always re-run a credit to pick it back up.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack, use the credits whenever your team is ready — no clock running against you.

Is WorkflowIQ included in a SOPBot pack?

No — it's a separate, pay-per-report purchase, run once you have SOPs worth analyzing.

What does a new hire need?

Just more call credits. Top up à la carte at $49/SOP, or step up to the next pack.

What if our process changes?

Re-run WorkflowIQ on the updated SOP for $49 to get a fresh report against the new version.
