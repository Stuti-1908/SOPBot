Questions

Before you call.

Do employees need to install anything?

No. It's a phone call to a regular number. No app, no login, no calendar invite.

What if they don't finish in one call?

Most calls run 15-30 minutes. If something's cut short, they can simply call back and walk through it again from the start.

Can one person cover more than one process in a single call?

Yes. If someone owns several processes worth documenting, they can walk through all of them in one call - each one is written up as its own section in their SOP document, so nothing gets mixed together.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack, use the credits whenever your team is ready - no clock running against you.

Is WorkflowIQ included in a SOPBot pack?

No - it's a separate, pay-per-report product. See WorkflowIQ pricing →
