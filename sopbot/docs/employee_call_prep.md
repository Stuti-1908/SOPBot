# Before You Call SOPBot

SOPBot is a short voice interview that documents how you do one part of your job, so your team has an accurate, up-to-date process guide - useful for training new hires and for making sure nothing lives only in your head.

**How it works:** Call **+1 903-626-7053** whenever you have 15-30 minutes free. It's a normal phone call with an AI interviewer - no forms, no typing, just talk it through like you're explaining your job to a new coworker.

## What you'll be asked

**1. A few quick basics**
Your company name, your first name, your job title, how long you've been in the role, and a one-sentence summary of what your role covers.

**2. Which process you're documenting**
You'll name one specific process you own (e.g. "processing weekly refunds," "onboarding a new client"), how often it happens, and what triggers it.

**3. The step-by-step walkthrough (the main part of the call)**
You'll walk through the process from start to finish, one step at a time. Expect follow-ups like:
- "What happens next?"
- "What tool or system do you use for that?"
- "Who else is involved?"
- "What could go wrong, and what do you do about it?"
- If a step varies ("it depends") - be ready to walk through each scenario separately.

**4. Wrapping up**
What marks the process as fully done, what usually causes it to go wrong, who receives the final output, and anything a brand-new person would likely get wrong the first time. You'll also confirm which tools, logins, and templates someone would need.

## How to prepare (optional but helpful)

You don't need to prepare anything formal, but the call goes faster if you've thought through:
- The **one process** you want to document (pick something you do regularly and know well)
- The **tools/systems** you touch during that process
- Any **edge cases or exceptions** that come up
- What **"done" looks like** for this process

## A few things to know

- There are no wrong answers - the interviewer just wants to understand exactly how you actually work, not how it's "supposed to" work on paper.
- It asks one question at a time and won't rush you.
- If you don't catch a question or need a second to think, that's fine - just say so.
- At the end, you'll get a chance to confirm everything sounds right before the call wraps up.
- A formatted SOP document based on your call will be shared with your manager afterward.
